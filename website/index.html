Our story

“Built around food we’d want to eat every day.”

EightyTwenty started in Canberra as a café for clean, natural, unprocessed food: breakfast, brunch and lunch you can eat every day, and EightyTwenty coffee worth getting up for. Ten cafés on, the idea hasn’t changed: 80% mindful, 20% indulgent, and a table where everyone’s welcome.

Two of our cafés, Belconnen and Phillip, are dedicated gluten-free kitchens, so coeliac guests can order anything on the menu.

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

EightyTwenty runs ten cafés across Canberra. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you order, book a table, request catering or contact us, and what we do with it. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, email address, the items you ordered, any notes or dietary requests, and the café you chose.
  • Delivery details: your delivery address, where you've asked for delivery.
  • Booking and catering details: your name, contact details, party size, date and time, and anything you tell us about the occasion.
  • Account details: if you create an account, the name, email and phone number you save with us, plus your order and booking history.
  • Website information: basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, collected through cookies. See our cookie policy.

We do not collect or store your full card number. Card payments are processed by our payment provider, and we only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded.

Why we collect it

We use your information to prepare and deliver your order, confirm and manage bookings, quote and run catering, respond to enquiries and complaints, keep our accounts, and meet food safety and tax obligations.

We only send marketing email if you have asked for it, and every message includes an unsubscribe link. Opting out of marketing does not affect order or booking confirmations, which we have to send you.

Who we share it with

We share what is needed with the services that help us operate: our online ordering platform, our payment provider, delivery partners when you order delivery, and the providers that host this site and send our email. Each is bound to use your information only to provide that service.

We never sell your personal information. We disclose it otherwise only where the law requires it.

How we store it

Your information is held on secure services with access limited to staff who need it. We keep order, booking and catering records for as long as we need them for the purpose they were collected and to meet our legal and tax obligations, then delete or de-identify them.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw consent to marketing at any time. Email info@eightytwentyfood.com.au and we'll respond within a reasonable time. If you're not satisfied with how we've handled a privacy matter, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Contact us

Privacy questions: info@eightytwentyfood.com.au. For anything about a specific order or booking, the café you ordered from is usually quickest. Numbers are on each location page.

Refund policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

If something isn't right, tell us. We'd rather fix it than have you leave unhappy. This policy sits alongside your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, which nothing here takes away.

Your consumer guarantees

Our food and drinks come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. If an order is not of acceptable quality, not fit for purpose, or not what you ordered, you are entitled to a replacement or a refund.

Changing or cancelling an order

Once you submit an order our kitchen gets straight to work, so orders cannot be changed or cancelled after they are placed. If you've made a mistake, call the café immediately. If we haven't started your order we'll do what we can.

We can't offer a refund for change of mind, for an item you ordered by mistake, or for an order that isn't collected.

If something is wrong with your order

Contact the café on the day, with your order number and a photo if you can. Where an item is missing, incorrect, or not of acceptable quality we will remake it or refund it, whichever you prefer.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Where a card surcharge or delivery fee was charged on a refunded item, that portion is refunded with it. Card refunds usually appear within a few business days depending on your bank.

Bookings and catering

Table bookings are free and you can change or cancel one by replying to your confirmation or calling the café. Please let us know as early as you can so we can release the table.

Catering is quoted per event. Because we order produce and roster staff ahead of time, deposits and cancellation terms are set out in writing on your quote and are what applies to your booking. Changes to numbers are welcome up to the cut-off stated there.

Gift cards

Gift cards can be used at any EightyTwenty café. They cannot be exchanged for cash or reloaded, and lost or stolen cards can't be replaced, except where the Australian Consumer Law requires otherwise. If a gift card was bought in error, contact us before it's used and we'll help.

How to make a claim

Call the café you ordered from, using the number on its location page, or email info@eightytwentyfood.com.au with your order number and what went wrong.

Accessibility

Last updated 20 September 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this site and sit down comfortably in our cafés. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, and we treat accessibility as ongoing work rather than a box to tick.

On this website

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, and focus is visible as you move through it.
  • Text is sized and coloured for readable contrast, and the layout reflows down to small screens without horizontal scrolling.
  • Photographs carry descriptive alternative text, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Movement and scroll animations are switched off automatically if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Menu items list dietary labels (GF, GF on request, DF, vegan and vegetarian) as text, not colour alone.

In our cafés

Our ten cafés are different buildings, so entry, seating and bathroom access vary from site to site. The quickest way to know what to expect is to call the café directly before you visit. Numbers and addresses are on each location page, and our team will tell you honestly what the space is like and help you plan a table.

Assistance animals are welcome in all of our cafés. If you'd like help ordering, reading the menu or carrying your tray, just ask any of our staff.

Dietary access

Our Phillip café is 100% gluten free and our Belconnen café is a dedicated gluten free kitchen. Across the other cafés we prepare gluten free, dairy free, vegan and vegetarian dishes in shared kitchens, so we can't guarantee an allergen-free environment. Please tell our team about an allergy before you order and we'll talk you through what we can do safely.

Tell us what isn't working

If you hit a barrier on this site or in one of our cafés, we want to hear about it. Email info@eightytwentyfood.com.au with what happened and the page or café involved, and we'll get back to you and fix what we can. If you need information from this site in another format, ask and we'll provide it.

Cookie policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device so it can remember things between pages and visits. This page explains which ones we use and how to turn them off. It should be read with our privacy policy.

What we use

Essential

Keep your cart, your chosen café and your order method while you move around the site, keep you signed in, and keep checkout secure. The site doesn't work without these, so they can't be switched off.

Preference

Remember small choices between visits, like the café you usually order from and whether you've dismissed a notice, so you don't have to set them again.

Analytics

Tell us how many people visit, which pages they use and where they run into trouble, reported in aggregate. We use this to fix the site, not to identify you.

Third parties

Some parts of the site are provided by others and set their own cookies: our online ordering and payment platform when you check out, embedded maps on location pages, and embedded social content. Those cookies are governed by each provider's own policy.

Managing cookies

Every browser lets you see the cookies a site has stored, delete them, and block new ones, usually under Settings, then Privacy. Private or incognito windows clear them when you close the window.

Blocking essential cookies will stop ordering and sign-in from working. Blocking preference and analytics cookies won't stop you using the site.

Changes and questions

If we add or remove cookies we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions about cookies or anything else on this site: info@eightytwentyfood.com.au.
